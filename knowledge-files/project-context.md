## Key Learnings & Principles

Discovered that Gemini Veo 3 has significant limitations with Arabic RTL text rendering, leading to the insight that hybrid workflows (AI video generation + post-production text overlay) often produce better results than attempting everything in one step. Learned that controversy-driven hooks and accusatory tones effectively stop scrolling behavior while providing immediate legal protection value. Understands that Instagram's 2025 algorithm prioritizes authenticity over polished content, with the first three seconds being critical for retention. Recognizes that result-first hooks in conversational Omani Arabic align perfectly with current algorithm preferences.

---

## Approach & Patterns

Uses a result-first hook strategy with conversational Omani Arabic dialect, avoiding excessive emoji usage to maintain authenticity. Employs "edutainment" approach - wrapping educational legal content in entertaining meme formats. For video generation, uses specific technical approaches: detailed camera positioning descriptions, avoiding generic terms like "POV camera," ending prompts with "No subtitles, no text overlay," and using quotation marks around exact text with ALL CAPS for better AI text rendering. Adapts trending meme formats specifically for real estate education while maintaining legal authority positioning.
